# Auto Repair Field Service

Field service for auto repair shops and garages, built on Expodo Field Service.

Log the vehicle, its mileage and VIN, follow the repair from intake to invoice,
and let customers track their job online. Ready-made job types with their
checklists (oil change, brake service, full service, diagnostic).
